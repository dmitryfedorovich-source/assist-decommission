Phase 2 follows the Reference Letters migration (Phase 1, go-live June 30, 2026). Compensation Letters will move to EPAM Docs (docs.epam.com) in a different model from Phase 1: instead of employees submitting individual requests, the India HR team generates letters in bulk for all eligible employees during annual review or event-driven compensation cycles.

Key Difference

Batch vs. Self-Service — How Phase 2 Differs from Phase 1

Feature Phase 1 — Reference Letters Phase 2 — Compensation Letters
Who initiates Individual employee (self-service) India HR team (batch operation)
Volume One letter per request All eligible employees processed in one batch
Trigger On-demand (employee need) Annual review cycle or event-driven compensation change
Employee action Fills in form fields, then downloads Receives notification, opens request, clicks Accept (date-stamped), downloads PAN-protected PDF
Data input method Employee fills form; HR data pre-populated from systems India HR uploads XLS with all employee data & letter settings
