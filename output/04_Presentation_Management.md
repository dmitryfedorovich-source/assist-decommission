# Assist Decommission: India Document Workflows
## Management Briefing

**Audience:** EPAM Management  
**Date:** May 2026  

---

## Slide 1 — The Situation

**EPAM Assist is being decommissioned.**

India colleagues rely on Assist today to request official HR letters — an important part of their employment documentation needs.

To ensure continuity of service, we are migrating these workflows to **EPAM Docs** — EPAM's established document management platform — before Assist is turned off.

---

## Slide 2 — What India Employees Use Today

India employees use Assist to request 6 types of official HR letters:

| Letter | Used For |
|---|---|
| Form 60 | Tax compliance declaration |
| Address Proof Letter | Bank, loan, and utility applications |
| Service Letter | Employment verification |
| Visa Processing Letter | Visa applications |
| Letter of Recommendation | Professional references |
| Relocation Letter | Internal office relocation approval |

These letters are a **routine, high-frequency need** for India employees.

---

## Slide 3 — Our Approach

**Move, don't rebuild.**

EPAM Docs already exists and supports document request workflows. We are **configuring Docs to handle these 6 letter types**, not building a new system.

| What stays the same | What changes |
|---|---|
| Employee experience: request a letter, download it | Platform: Assist → Docs |
| HRBP review and approval process | URL and entry point |
| Letter content and format | — |
