27/03/26 Assist / DOCS – Compensation Letters & Related HR Letters
MFU from 27/03/2026

Key Clarifications & Decisions

1. Compensation Letter Structure

  • A single Compensation Letter template is used.
  • Letter content is conditionally rendered based on values and Yes/No selections from the uploaded Excel file.
  • Fixed text blocks remain the same for all employees; only populated sections appear dynamically.

2. Data Sources

  • Excel file: Primary source for compensation figures, bonus values, and conditional flags. Data is manually prepared by HR based on internal approvals (e.g. HRBP emails).
  • People Portal: Legal first/last name, Employee ID, Job title (designation), Personal (home) address.
  • Compensation Portal is not used as a direct automated source for these letters.

3. Numeric Values & Wording

  • HR provides amounts in digits only via Excel.
  • Assist automatically converts numbers to amount-in-words and ensures consistency between numeric and textual values.

4. Excel Logic – Letter Types

  • Excel rows 35–40 define the type of Compensation Letter (salary hike, promotion, OTB, etc.).
  • Exactly one option must be selected; otherwise upload fails.
  • Selected option determines which sections and clauses appear in the letter.

5. Bonuses & Variable Components

  • Excel rows 41–70 control: On-Target Bonus (OTB), Retention Bonuses (RB), Deferred Bonuses (DB), One-time / spot bonuses.
  • Values may or may not be present; only populated fields are rendered.
  • Payment schedules are shown in tabular form when applicable.

6. Employee Acknowledgement

  • Employees view letters in Assist → My Documents → Compensation Letters.
  • Available action: Accept (acknowledgement only). No reject or discussion flow available.
  • Visibility: Employee: Yes; Admin / Compensation team: Yes (status tracking); Resource Manager: No access.

7. Approvals

  • No additional approvals (e.g. Resource Manager) are required before issuing Compensation Letters or Reference/proof letters.

8. Reference / Proof Letters

  • Largely static templates. Employee address taken from People Portal → Personal Address (not office address).
  • Letter content does not vary by requester. No approval flow required.

3. Separation Letters (Resignation Acceptance & Experience)

4. Roles & Access

  • Employees can only see their own letters.

Key Discussion Points

1. Compensation Letter Scenarios

  • Compensation letters are issued for: Salary hike only; Salary hike with promotion; Salary hike with bonuses (OTB, RB, DB, One-Time); Salary hike with LTI.
  • Different scenarios use different letter formats, selected via XLS flags.

2. Data Sources and Automation

  • Excel upload file provides: all numeric values (CTC, salary components, bonuses); dates (effective dates, payout dates); logical flags (Yes / No).
  • Assist system: applies templates and wording; includes or excludes sections automatically; generates password-protected PDF letters.
  • People Portal is the source of truth for employee designation.

3. Logical Flags and Letter Control

  • XLS logical flags (Yes / No) control: Promotion type (P0–P4); Bonus applicability (OTB, RB, DB, One-Time); Recovery terms; Promotion eligibility.
  • Assist uses these flags only to render content, not to calculate values.

4. Recovery Terms

  • Recovery terms are applicable separately for: Retention Bonus; Deferred Bonus; One-Time Bonus. XLS values: Yes / No.
  • Assist inserts standard recovery clauses when enabled.

5. Employee Experience

  • Employee receives a notification to access Assist.
  • Letter is available in Assist only (not attached to email), provided as a password-protected PDF.
  • If marked "Action Required", employee must acknowledge the letter.

6. Decisions Made

  • Assist is confirmed as a letter generation and distribution tool — not an approval or calculation system.
  • Excel is confirmed as the single input source for all compensation data.
  • Letter wording and structure remain system-controlled and standardised.

Next Steps

23/04/26 To clarify current Assist/Docs processes for compensation-related bonuses and for separation letters (Resignation Acceptance / Service Letter and Experience Letter), focusing on automation, data sources, manual steps, and employee communication.
MFU from 23/04/2026

Key Clarifications & Decisions

1. Compensation Letters – Bonus Amounts Calculation

  • Deferred Bonus: total amount not stored/shown in a single field. System automatically calculates total based on scheduled instalments. Total is derived automatically (Assist).
  • Retention Bonus & One-time Bonus: total amount is explicitly provided. System bifurcates total into scheduled payments. Total is manually defined, then split (XLS).

2. Types of Separation Letters

  • Service Letter (Resignation Acceptance / Relieving Letter): generated on last working day; confirms employment period and general conduct; used mainly for immediate background verification.
  • Experience Letter: generated after full & final settlement (typically ~10–15 days post-exit); released only after Finance confirms settlement is complete.

3. Employee Contact Details (Personal Email & Phone)

  • Personal email and phone originally collected at joining and stored in People/DOCS systems.
  • Reconfirmed at exit because corporate email becomes inactive after separation.
  • Reconfirmation via Excel form emailed to employee — not done via Assist portal. If no response, HR uses previously stored data from DOCS/People Portal.

4. Trigger and Automation Level for Separation Letters

  • Resignations marked in People Portal; HR downloads exit lists (Excel) from People Portal daily.
  • Assist automation: employee details auto-populated from People Portal after entering Employee ID; letter templates pre-configured in Assist.
  • Manual action required: select employee, enter/update personal contact details, click Send/Release.
  • No end-to-end automatic generation or sending without HR manual intervention.

5. Letter Delivery to Ex-Employees

  • After exit, employees do not have access to Assist.
  • Both Service Letter and Experience Letter sent via email as PDF attachments (not links) to personal email address.

6. Tracking of Experience Letter Timing

  • Finance shares a settlement clearance file with HR.
  • HR maintains a manual tracking Excel; once marked as paid, HR manually sends Experience Letter via Assist.

1. Nominee Details & Statutory Forms

  • Legal source: Statutory forms (Form F, Form 2) remain the legal source of truth for nominee data. Updates in Assist are for operational reference only — they have no legal effect on their own.
  • Statutory forms are immutable: Once Form F / Form 2 is submitted, it cannot be updated. Any subsequent nominee changes are tracked in Assist or Docs for operational purposes only.
  • 50% data gap: Only approx. 50% of India employees have nominee data in the Assist portal. The remaining 50% are covered by PF forms and gratuity forms collected during onboarding.
  • Current use: India team cross-validates nominee allocation percentages across gratuity Form F, PF forms, and Assist portal. Discrepancies are resolved on a case-by-case basis (typically at exit / death event).
  • Root cause of duplication: Employees cannot update statutory forms after initial submission; Assist portal was built as an operational workaround to capture nominee updates over time.
  • Agreed path forward: Explore enabling employee self-service nominee updates in Docs, with a one-time bulk migration of current Assist nominee data to Docs as a migration action. (Action: Darya, Hanna, Satish to investigate feasibility — Docs team + legal to confirm disclaimer requirements.)

2. Separation Model

  • Resignation form fields confirmed: On submission, only Date of Resignation carries over to the exit request. Exit category and comments are added separately by the RM or People Partner.
  • Notice period calculation: Calculated automatically by grade — A4 grade = 60 days, A5 grade = 90 days. Formula: date of resignation + notice period days = tentative last working day as per policy.
  • Three fields needed for analysis:
    • Last Working Day per Policy: calculated from grade-based notice period; used per-employee for exit processing.
    • Notice Period Days Remaining: used to calculate severance pay (RTB / termination / PIP cases) — not for reporting.
    • Revocation Status: tracks how many exit requests were cancelled in a given month — used for Power BI reporting.
  • RM approval flow: Once employee submits resignation, RM (and unit head) receive notification. RM negotiates last working day and approves the "Last Working Day Approved" date in People Portal. This approved date is the only field that matters for downstream processing. Rejection requires a brief comment (used for retention tracking only).
  • Employee self-service on People platform: Satish confirmed employees do not currently have access to raise resignation requests directly. Adding this as a People Platform feature (rather than a separate service request) was discussed as the preferred approach. Pending review of data privacy and audit implications.
  • Notice period Excel: HRBPs maintain a manual tracking Excel for notice period clearance (payables and recoveries). This is internal between teams and not connected to any portal.

3. Compensation Letters

  • Acceptance mechanism: A radio button "Accept" is required. Once clicked, it becomes disabled and records the date/time of acceptance. No penalty for non-acceptance; no reject option. Employee cannot dispute the letter in the system — any concerns must be raised with their RM directly. Decision D-P2-03 confirmed
  • Security — PAN password protection: PAN-protected PDF is the primary requirement. If Docs cannot implement PDF password protection, fallback is: (a) a disclaimer/caution note displayed before document access, (b) watermarking on the letter template. Satish to provide the caution note content. (Action: Satish — provide disclaimer/caution note text; Docs team + Legal — validate wording.)
  • Letter types — 4 only: Only 4 compensation letter types need to be migrated from Assist to Docs. Flex-to-fixed letter variants are no longer required — those employees were moved to fixed after GDO merge effective 1 April 2026. Decision D-P2-06 confirmed
  • Status & auto-close: Letter status moves to "generated" once the letter becomes visible to the employee in Docs. Request auto-closes after 1 week. No additional reporting required beyond this. Decision D-P2-07/08 confirmed
  • Indian currency format: Confirmed — all compensation letter amounts must use Indian currency format (INR, lakh/crore notation). Decision confirmed

07/05/26 Discuss nominee details from 04/05/2026 (by Hanna Vasilenka)
MFU from 07/05/2026

Key Discussions & Agreements

1. Nominee Details — Scope & Assist Decommission Impact

  • Nominee details currently stored in Assist portal are operational reference only — they carry no legal weight. Statutory forms (Form F for gratuity, Form 2 for PF) remain the authoritative legal source.
  • Once statutory forms are submitted at onboarding, they cannot be updated. Any subsequent nominee changes made in Assist exist solely as an operational override and are cross-validated by the India HR team against the statutory forms when needed (typically at exit or death claim).
  • Approximately 50% of India employees have nominee data recorded in Assist. The remaining 50% are covered only by PF/gratuity forms from their onboarding package.
  • When Assist is decommissioned, this operational nominee data will be lost unless explicitly migrated. The 50% coverage gap must be factored into the migration plan.

2. Proposed Solution: Self-Service in Docs + One-Time Bulk Migration

  • The agreed direction is to enable employees to update nominee details via a self-service request in Docs platform, providing a structured, versioned record that India HR can access at any time.
  • A one-time bulk migration of current Assist nominee data to Docs is required as part of the Assist decommission, so existing records are not lost.
  • After migration, all employees will be prompted to review and reconfirm their nominee information in Docs as part of the cutover communication.
  • The self-service process will include appropriate disclaimers (to be confirmed with Legal) clarifying that Docs data is operational and does not replace statutory forms.

3. Key Constraints Confirmed

  • Nominee details in Docs are not a replacement for statutory forms — the India team continues to rely on gratuity Form F and PF Form 2 as legal documents.
  • The Docs process must support version tracking (date-stamped updates) so India HR can see the most recent declared nominee allocation percentage.
  • RM does not need access to nominee data — this is a People Ops / India HR function only.
