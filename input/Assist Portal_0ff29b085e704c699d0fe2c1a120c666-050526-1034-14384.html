Assist Portal  
Project Overview:  
The purpose of Assist project is to develop a portal which will help Epam India HR Business Unit to process the Associate(s) requests and  
minimize the manual effort of HR services.  
HR Services includes issuing  
Reference Letters such as Address proof Letter, Service Letter, Visa Processing Letter,LOR (Letter Of Recommendation),Relocation  
Letter  
Compensation Letters such as Offer Letters,Mid Term Hike Letters,Annual Hike Letters,Variable Pay Letters,Retention Bonus Letters,  
LTI,One Time Bonus Letter,Pay Slips,Form-16  
Separation Letters such as No Due Forms,Relieving Letters,Experience Letter  
Maintaining Personal Records of Associates such as New Joiner Documents,Govt. ID documents,Previous employment,PF,Nomination  
Forms,AEWS,Daily Letters,Compensation Letters,Separation Letters,Warning Letters  
The scope of MVP is to develop Assist portal to serve the associate requests to generate Reference Letters.  
Reference Letters includes - Address proof Letter, Service Letter, Visa Processing Letter,LOR (Letter Of Recommendation)  
The Scope of MVP1 is to generate Compensation Letters through Assist portal <<TBD>>
