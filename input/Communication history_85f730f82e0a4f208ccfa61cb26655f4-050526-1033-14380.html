/No selections from the uploaded Excel file.

Legal first/last name

HR provides amounts in digits only via Excel.  
Assist automatically:  
Converts numbers to amountinwords

Excel rows 35–40 define the type of Compensation Letter  
(salary hike, promotion, OTB, etc.).  
Exactly one option must be selected; otherwise, upload fails.  
Selected option determines which sections and clauses appear  
in the letter.

Excel rows 41–70 control:  
OnTarget Bonus (OTB)  
Retention Bonuses (RB)  
Deferred Bonuses (DB)  
Onetime / spot bonuses

rendered.  
Payment schedules are shown in tabular form when applicable.

Visibility:

Resource Manager: No access to compensation details.

before issuing:

Reference / proof letters discussed later.

1. Compensation letters – bonus amounts calculation  
Deferred Bonus  
The total amount is not explicitly stored or shown in a  
single field in the letter.  
The system automatically calculates the total based on  
scheduled instalments (e.g., August/December values).  
Retention Bonus & One-time Bonus  
The total amount is explicitly provided.  
The system then bifurcates this total into scheduled  
payments.  
Diana  
Nikoghosyan  
Dzmitry  
Fedarovich  
Conclusion:  
Automation differs by bonus type:  
Deferred → total is derived automatically (Assist).  
Retention /One-time → total is manually defined  
and then split (XLS).  
2. Types of separation letters  
Two different letters are generated during exit:  
1. Service Letter (Resignation Acceptance / Relieving Letter)  
Generated on the last working day.  
Confirms employment period and general conduct.  
Used mainly for immediate background  
verification.  
2. Experience Letter  
Generated after full & final settlement (typically  
~10–15 days post-exit).  
Released only after confirmation from Finance that  
the settlement is complete.  
3. Employee contact details (personal email & phone)  
Personal email and phone are:  
Originally collected at joining and stored in People/DOCS  
systems.  
Reconfirmed at exit because the corporate email  
becomes inactive after separation.  
Reconfirmation process:  
Employees receive an email with an Excel form to  
reconfirm details.  
This is not done via ASSIST portal.  
If no response, HR uses previously stored data from  
DOCS/People Portal.  
4. Trigger and automation level for separation letters  
Trigger source:  
Resignations are marked in People Portal.  
HR downloads exit lists (Excel) from the People Portal  
daily.  
ASSIST automation level:  
Employee details (name, designation, etc.) are auto-  
populated from People Portal after entering the Employee  
ID.  
Letter templates are pre-configured in ASSIST.  
Manual action is required to:  
Select the employee.  
Enter/update personal contact details.  
Click Send/Release.  
There is no end-to-end automatic generation or sending without  
HR manual intervention.  
5. Letter delivery to ex-employees  
After exit, employees do not have access to ASSIST.  
Both Service Letter and Experience Letter are:  
Sent via email.  
Provided as PDF attachments (not links).  
Emails are sent to:  
Personal email address for ex-employees.  
6. Tracking of Experience Letter timing  
Finance shares a settlement clearance file with HR.  
HR maintains a manual tracking Excel:  
Settlement status is updated.  
Once marked as paid, HR manually sends the  
Experience Letter via ASSIST.
